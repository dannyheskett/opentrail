# Privacy Policy — OpenTrail

_Last updated: October 22, 2026_

OpenTrail ("the app") is a single-player game. This policy explains, plainly,
what the app does with your information: **nothing.**

## Data we collect

**None.** OpenTrail does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal
information or usage data. Specifically:

- The app requests **no permissions** and has **no internet access** — it
  cannot send anything off your device.
- There are **no accounts, logins, or sign-ins.**
- There is **no analytics, telemetry, advertising, or third-party SDKs.**
- The names you type, your Top Ten and your party's graves stay on your
  device and are never transmitted.

## Children

OpenTrail collects no data from anyone, so it collects no data from children.

## Third parties

OpenTrail contains no third-party services, ad networks, or trackers, and does
not share data with anyone (there is no data to share).

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL
with a revised "Last updated" date.
